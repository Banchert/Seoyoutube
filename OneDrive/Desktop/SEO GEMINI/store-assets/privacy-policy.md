# Privacy Policy - YouTube AI Optimizer

**Last updated: November 28, 2024**

## Overview
YouTube AI Optimizer ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Chrome extension.

## Information We Collect

### Authentication Data
- Google OAuth tokens for YouTube API access
- YouTube channel information (channel ID, name)
- Video metadata (titles, descriptions, thumbnails)

### Usage Data
- Extension usage statistics (anonymous)
- Error logs for debugging purposes
- Performance metrics

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Functions
- Authenticate with YouTube API
- Retrieve your video information
- Generate optimized content using AI
- Update your video metadata
- Upload custom thumbnails

### Service Improvement
- Analyze usage patterns to improve features
- Debug issues and enhance performance
- Provide customer support

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- Authentication tokens are stored locally in your browser
- No sensitive data is transmitted to external servers
- All data processing happens on your device

### Security Measures
- Secure OAuth 2.0 authentication
- Encrypted data transmission
- No permanent storage of personal data

## Data Sharing

### We DO NOT:
- Sell your personal information
- Share your data with third parties
- Store your content on our servers
- Access your data without permission

### We MAY Share:
- Anonymous usage statistics for service improvement
- Technical data with Google for API compliance
- Legal information if required by law

## Your Rights

### You Can:
- Revoke access at any time through Google Account settings
- Delete all stored data by uninstalling the extension
- Request information about your data usage
- Contact us with privacy concerns

### Data Retention
- Authentication tokens: Until you revoke access
- Usage logs: 30 days maximum
- Error reports: 90 days maximum

## Third-Party Services

### Google APIs
- We use YouTube Data API v3 for video management
- Subject to Google's Privacy Policy
- Data usage complies with Google API Terms

### AI Services
- Content generation may use external AI APIs
- No personal data is sent to AI services
- Only video metadata is processed

## Children's Privacy
This extension is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Users will be notified of significant changes through the extension or our website.
